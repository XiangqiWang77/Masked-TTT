Figure 1. Training-pool selection at the common warmup checkpoint. Blue dashed contours include all 573 query-specific trajectories; orange solid contours include the 390 retained by ED-JSD. Crosses show all 183 rejected trajectories. Coordinates are trajectory-mean top-k-plus-rest bucket entropies; the diagonal is equal mean entropy, not the gate boundary. Contours enclose 50% and 80% of separately normalized, shared-bandwidth reflected KDEs; they are not confidence intervals. These are selection-pool distributions, not final model probability distributions.

Figure 2. Paired final-answer transitions on the same 32 completed BABILong requests using query-specific data, matched Teacher targets, initial KV, and update budgets. The cohort contains seven recorded development and 25 recorded holdout requests. Circle area encodes count: 24 remain wrong, six remain correct, two are corrected, and none regress. Accuracy rises from 18.75% to 25.00%. This partial historical cohort does not establish a non-regression guarantee or a result for the three-dataset experiment.
