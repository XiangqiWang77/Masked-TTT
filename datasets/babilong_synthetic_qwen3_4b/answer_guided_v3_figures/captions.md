# Figure captions

**Figure 1 | Answer-guided query-specific self-study targets Dilution and Fragmentation.** Query-agnostic, block-local generation can spread a fixed training budget across unrelated topics and leave complementary evidence disconnected. Contextual Cartridges first selects query anchors and bridge blocks, extracts an answer or useful intermediate conclusion, and reverses it into a closed-book question. The schematic contains no empirical values.

**Figure 2 | Observed wall time for two completed 1,000-case synthetic-data campaigns.** Bars show median seconds per case at each document length; the empirical cumulative distributions retain all completed cases; ratios above one indicate a lower median for query-specific generation. The overall median is 131.5 s for question-agnostic generation and 71.1 s for query-specific generation (descriptive ratio 1.85×). Campaigns used different concurrency and backend schedules, and query-specific case time includes shared-batch waiting; ratios are descriptive, not a controlled causal speedup.

**Figure 3 | Query-specific generation produces more focused and support-covering synthetic data across document length.** Overall bars report case-level means for query-term focus, support-recall lower bound, and complete-support-chain exposure lower bound. Length panels show support recall, complete-chain exposure, and the mean evidence footprint for 250 cases per length and method. Six question-agnostic support-recall values are unavailable and remain missing. These are data-construction proxies, not semantic correctness or downstream Cartridge accuracy.
